02 — Case Studies

Production Modules

Each project follows a Goal → Challenge → Solution → Impact framework. These are real products shipped to real users.

Android Native · Clean Architecture

Quran Kareem

Offline-first Quran companion with real-time Qibla compass, prayer timetables, audio recitation, translation overlays, and Hijri calendar — all computed locally.

Challenge
Prayer calculations returned null at polar latitudes where twilight never occurs.
Solution & Impact
Clamped GPS latitude to ±65° and applied HighLatitudeRule.MIDDLE_OF_THE_NIGHT fallback. Zero crash reports from polar regions.
