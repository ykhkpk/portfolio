PDFEditor

Professional iPhone & iPad vector PDF document editor featuring true in-place content editing of existing PDF text, fonts, colors, and graphic objects with low-level page reconstruction.

Challenge

Quran Kareem

High-performance offline-first Islamic companion for iOS and Android featuring 14-line Mushaf reader, real-time sensor-fused Qibla compass, prayer timetables, and audio recitation.

Challenge
Executing concurrent queries across 6,236 Ayahs and translations without UI stutter or data races, plus polar latitude calculation singularities.

AetherChat

Decentralized, offline-first peer-to-peer mesh messaging and WebRTC calling application operating over local Bluetooth and Wi-Fi networks without internet, cellular, or centralized servers.

Challenge
Providing reliable, verified encrypted communication and transactional state sync across ad-hoc radio meshes subject to sudden peer drop-offs.
Solution & Impact
