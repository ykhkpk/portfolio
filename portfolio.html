About Me

Building Apps

I'm a results-driven Mobile Application Developer based in Pakistan, specializing in Android Native (Kotlin) and Flutter (Dart). I've spent 5+ years architecting, building, and shipping production-grade mobile applications.

My work ranges from complex sensor-based utility apps (real-time GPS compasses, speedometers) to AI/LLM-powered solutions, creative design tools, and real-time messaging platforms. I'm passionate about Clean Architecture, performance optimization, and delivering pixel-perfect user experiences.

I strictly follow SOLID principles, MVVM patterns, and leverage tools like Hilt DI, Room DB, and Riverpod to build scalable, maintainable codebases.
